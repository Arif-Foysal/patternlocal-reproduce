# PatternLocal reproduction — root-cause notes

## Why PatternLocal was not beating LIME (fixed 2026-10-19)

### Symptom
With the original settings (`K_RATIO = 0.05`, auto bandwidth) PatternLocal was
*equal to or worse than* LIME on XOR (WHITE and CORR) across all SNRs, e.g.
XOR WHITE alpha=0.9: LIME EMD 0.102 / IME 0.64 vs PatternLocal EMD 0.121 /
IME 0.74.  The paper has PatternLocal clearly best, especially on IME.

### Root cause
The local covariance in Eq. (7) is `C = sum_i v_i (x_i - mu)(x_i - mu)^T`
over the kernel-weighted neighbourhood.  With `k_ratio = 0.05` the top-5%
nearest neighbours of any instance are **all from the same class**: for
XAI-TRIS every image is `x = alpha * (+-)s + (1 - alpha) * eta` with `s` a fixed
per-class signal, so same-class pairs sit at distance ~0.14 while other-class
pairs sit at ~1.8.  The k nearest neighbours therefore share the *identical*
signal, and the local covariance has **~zero variance along the signal
direction**.  `a = C w` then projects the LIME weights onto the noise subspace
and destroys the attribution.

This is not a bug in the maths — it is the correct behaviour of a local
covariance, but it is the wrong neighbourhood for these data.

### Fix
Use the kernel over the **whole training set** (`k_ratio = 1.0`), which is the
paper's Eq. (6)/(7) expectation over `P_X` and the upstream default.  Confirm
by the effect being bandwidth-insensitive there (auto ~ 1.8 and 30 and
`use_global_cov=True` all agree to 4 decimals).

| fixture (10 samples) | LIME | PL k=0.05 | PL k=1.0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| XOR WHITE a=0.5 | 0.1129 / 0.6936 | 0.1218 / 0.7398 | **0.0739 / 0.0349** |
| XOR WHITE a=0.9 | 0.1027 / 0.6422 | 0.1209 / 0.7375 | **0.0645 / 0.0037** |
| XOR CORR  a=0.5 | 0.1125 / 0.6931 | 0.0989 / 0.6513 | **0.0571 / 0.1604** |
| XOR CORR  a=0.9 | 0.1027 / 0.6460 | 0.1554 / 0.6676 | **0.1300 / 0.0063** |

Localization on the saved qualitative samples (4 instances, alpha=0.9):
`mass_inside_mask` LIME 0.36, PatternLocal 0.994, Analytical-IG 0.993.

### Other fixes made at the same time
1. **PatternLocal without forming C.** `Cw` is computed with three BLAS gemvs
   instead of an `O(N D^2)` matmul:
   `mu = v @ X; t = X @ w - mu @ w; Cw = (v * t) @ X`.
   0.28 s/sample at N=40000, D=4096 (was ~2-5 s), and no 655 MB temporaries.
   Verified against a float64 reference: rel. err ~1e-6.
2. **`AnalyticalPatternLocalExplainer._cov_transform`** does the same for the
   analytical (gradient / IG) methods.
3. **KNN cache keyed by `id()` was unsound** — per-instance `(1, D)`
   temporaries are freed each iteration and CPython reuses their addresses, so
   the cache could hand back a *previous* instance's neighbourhood.  Now keyed
   by content (`tobytes()`).
4. **`max_k` was 300 while the k-grid searched up to 800**, so k=500/800
   silently used 300 neighbours.  The evaluator now queries all N neighbours.
5. **Analytical k-grid extended** to `{100, 1000, 5000, N}` — the large-k end
   is the full-data covariance, the same regime that fixes PatternLocal.
6. **IG was recomputed inside every k evaluation**; now computed once per
   instance and shared by the k-search and the final evaluation.
7. **Threaded EMD scoring** (`SCORE_WORKERS=4`); POT's network simplex releases
   the GIL, ~2-2.5x on the scoring phase.

### Hyperparameter search
`PAT_K_GRID = (0.05, 0.5, 1.0)` (the tune set reliably picks 1.0),
`LIME_BW_GRID = (10, 20, 30)`, analytical `k in {100, 1000, 5000, N}`.
Bandwidth / lambda / kernel are inert once the explanation is normalised.

## Sweep restructure: 9 single-alpha parts (2026-10-20)

The SNR sweeps for all 6 scenarios are now **9 parts each, one alpha per part**
(`sweep_1` = alpha 0.1 ... `sweep_9` = alpha 0.9), replacing the previous
5-part layout (parts 1-4 held two alphas, part 5 held one).

* Templates: 54 files regenerated by `scripts/split_sweeps_into_9.py`
  (`templates/{xor,lin,rigid}_evaluation/<scenario>_sweep/<scenario>_sweep_1..9.py`).
* Config: `scripts/rewrite_sweep_config.py` replaced the 30 old
  `phase3_<scenario>_sweep_<n>` blocks with 54 new ones.
* Kernel slug convention unchanged: `<scenario>-sweep-<n>`
  (e.g. `patternlocal-repro-xor-corr-sweep-6`).
* Every part mounts its scenario's data-gen kernel via `kernel_sources`.
* The core evaluator now also runs the paper's baseline set (Sec. 4.3):
  Saliency, DeepLift (Rescale), GradientShap, GuidedBackprop, Sobel, Laplace
  (13 methods total), and the analytical k-grid is floored at the
  PatternLocal-selected k to stop the tuner picking a structurally-wrong small k.
